# BioMed Analyzer X200

## Overview
The **Analyzer X200** is a benchtop, random-access clinical chemistry analyzer designed for mid-to-high volume clinical pathology laboratories. It operates at an analytical throughput of up to 800 photometric tests per hour (1,200 tests/hour with optional ISE electrolyte module).

## Physical Architecture
The instrument is partitioned into six core functional subsystems:
- **[[Fluidics]]**: High-precision hydraulic aspiration and dispensing manifold.
- **[[Optics]]**: Photometer with a 12-wavelength holographic diffraction grating.
- **[[Robotics]]**: Dual independent pipettor arms with capacitive liquid level detection (LLD).
- **[[Thermal]]**: Direct-contact reaction carousel incubation bath maintained at 37.0°C ± 0.1°C.
- **[[Power]]**: Multi-rail 24V DC switch-mode power distribution.
- **[[Consumables]]**: Automated cuvette feeder and continuous-load reagent carousel.

## Diagnostic Standards
- Baseline hydraulic manifold pressure: `120 kPa ± 5%` (114.0 to 126.0 kPa).
- Photometer baseline optical absorbance: `< 0.005 AU` at 340nm.
- Pipettor home position repeatable accuracy: `± 0.05 mm`.
