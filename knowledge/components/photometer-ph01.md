# Diffraction Grating Photometer Assembly PH-01

## Functional Role
The **PH-01 Photometer** is the primary optical measurement engine of the [[Analyzer-X200]]. It utilizes a high-blazed holographic concave diffraction grating and a 12-channel silicon photodiode array to measure absorbance across 340 nm – 800 nm.
