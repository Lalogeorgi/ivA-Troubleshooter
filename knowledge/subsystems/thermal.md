# Thermal Incubation & Climate Subsystem

## Description
The Thermal subsystem stabilizes reaction kinetics by maintaining the reaction carousel at 37.0°C ± 0.1°C via silicone resistive heating mats and Peltier thermoelectric modules for reagent compartment refrigeration (4.0°C to 10.0°C).
