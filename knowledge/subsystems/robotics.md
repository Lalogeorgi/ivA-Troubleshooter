# Robotic Pipettor & Motion Subsystem

## Description
The Robotics subsystem orchestrates precision X-Y-Z and rotary positioning of the primary sample pipettor and secondary reagent dispensing arms. Incorporates high-resolution optical encoders and high-frequency capacitive liquid-level sensing (LLD).

## Specifications
- Arm Trajectory Repeatability: ± 0.05 mm
- Liquid Level Detection Sensitivity: Down to 50 µL serum in standard 13x75mm tubes
- Clot Detection: Pressure-profile differential sensing during aspiration
