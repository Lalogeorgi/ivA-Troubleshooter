# Power Distribution & Mains Subsystem

## Description
The Power Distribution Subsystem conditions incoming mains AC (100–240V AC, 50/60 Hz) and outputs stabilized DC rails (+24V for motors and pumps, +12V for lamps and cooling fans, +5V and +3.3V for microcontrollers and FPGA logic).
