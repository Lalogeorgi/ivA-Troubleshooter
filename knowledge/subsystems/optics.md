# Optical Detection Subsystem

## Description
The Optical Detection subsystem performs multi-wavelength spectrophotometric measurement across 12 discrete optical wavelengths (340 nm to 800 nm). It comprises a stabilized tungsten-halogen light source, optical fiber bundle, motorized diffraction grating, and high-sensitivity silicon photodiode array.

## Specifications
- Wavelength Range: 340, 380, 405, 450, 480, 505, 546, 570, 600, 660, 700, 800 nm
- Photometric Linearity: 0.000 to 3.200 AU (± 1%)
- Source Lamp: 12V 50W Quartz Tungsten-Halogen (Lifespan ~2000 hours)
- Dark Current Drift: < 0.0005 AU/hr
