# Service Bulletin SB-2026-014

## Title
Updated Manifold Pressure Calibration Standards and Seal Replacement for FW 4.2+

## Classification
- **Bulletin Number**: SB-2026-014
- **Release Date**: March 15, 2026
- **Target Audience**: Field Service Engineers, Technical Support Specialists
- **Urgency**: Mandatory during next scheduled PM or if error [[E1045]] occurs

---

## 1. Issue Description
With the release of Firmware 4.2.0, the sampling cycle speed of [[Analyzer-X200]] was accelerated by 18% to support high-throughput clinical batches. 

Under continuous duty cycles, hydraulic pulse resonance can cause the earlier rev-A plunger seal in [[Pump-P102]] to experience premature micro-fissuring, causing sporadic pressure drops below `114.0 kPa` and tripping error [[E1045]]. 

Furthermore, legacy pressure sensors [[Pressure-Sensor-PS23]] manufactured prior to Lot #2025Q3 exhibited a thermal offset drift of up to +3.5 kPa when internal bay temperature exceeds 34°C.

## 2. Corrective Field Actions
Field Service Engineers servicing instruments running Firmware 4.2.0 or later must perform the following:
1. Verify the installed revision of [[Pump-P102]]. If equipped with Black Viton seals, replace immediately with the upgraded PTFE/Kalrez composite seal kit [[SP-948-230-01]].
2. Recalibrate [[Pressure-Sensor-PS23]] using the 2-point NIST calibration curve detailed in [[PROC-PUMP-CALIBRATION]].
3. Update EEPROM parameter `P_DAMPING_FACTOR` from default `0.85` to `0.92` to filter high-frequency hydraulic resonance.

## 3. Required Parts & Tooling
- Seal Upgrade Kit: [[SP-948-230-01]] (Qty: 1)
- Pressure Sensor Rev-C (if Lot < 2025Q3): [[SP-948-510-23]] (Qty: 1)
- Digital Manometer: `948-CAL-01`
- Hex Tool 2.5 mm

## 4. Warranty & Labor Reimbursement
- Standard warranty reimbursement: 1.5 hours field labor code `FL-FLUID-014`.
- Replaced parts must be tagged with RMA and returned to BioMed Quality Assurance within 30 days.
