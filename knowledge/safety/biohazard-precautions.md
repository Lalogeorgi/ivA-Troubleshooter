# Clinical Biohazard Decontamination & Protection Protocols

## Scope
This safety protocol is mandatory for all Field Service Engineers servicing the [[Analyzer-X200]] fluidics, probe mechanics, waste lines, or sample incubation carousels.

> [!CAUTION] Biological Hazard Alert
> All patient biological samples (whole blood, serum, plasma, urine, cerebrospinal fluid) must be treated as potentially infectious for HIV, HBV, HCV, and other bloodborne pathogens.

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements
1. **Gloves**: Double-glove with heavy-duty nitrile gloves (minimum thickness 5 mil). Change gloves immediately if punctured or contaminated.
2. **Eye/Face Protection**: ANSI Z87.1 certified safety glasses with side shields, or full face shield when servicing pressurized fluidics.
3. **Lab Coat / Gown**: Fluid-resistant disposable laboratory gown with cuffed sleeves.

## Pre-Intervention Decontamination Protocol
1. **Automated Line Flush**: Run the automated decontamination cycle using 0.5% sodium hypochlorite (10% laboratory bleach dilution) followed by a 5-minute deionized water rinse.
2. **Surface Decontamination**: Wipe all exterior panels, sample carousel trays, and pipettor needles with 70% isopropanol or quaternary ammonium disinfectant wipes.
3. **Fluid Spill Protocol**: In case of active leaks, immediately place absorbent pads containing sodium dichloroisocyanurate granules.

## Safe Waste Disposal
- All soiled wipes, tubing cutoffs, syringe seals ([[SP-948-230-01]]), and disposables must be discarded in rigid biohazard containers labeled with the universal biohazard symbol.
- Never recap contaminated sample needles or pipettor tips by hand.
